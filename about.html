02 — About

17.Building anyway.

Founder. Builder. Occasionally a thinker. Mostly just someone who ships more than he talks — which, given the industry, is a competitive advantage.

I grew up in a business family. My father runs his own. So entrepreneurship was never a career decision — it was the air.

What I had to choose was the tool. I picked AI, distribution, and speed — because in 2024, those three combined were the shortest distance between an idea and a real business.

I started building seriously in 2025. Dropped out of college in the same year. That's not a badge — it's just the honest answer to “how do you have time for all this?”. I don't. I made time by removing the thing that wasn't working for me.

A year later, Genartml is a team building AI systems for companies that don't want to wait for enterprise software to catch up. Alongside it: Extuter, an AI learning companion for JEE and NEET aspirants, and Evoluter, a UPSC prep platform. I'm 17.

I write here occasionally when I have something honest to say, and I take a small number of meetings when the reason is clear. Everything else — announcements, jargon, thought-leadership — I try to skip. There's already enough of that.

What I actually believe
Six operating principles
01
Building beats talking.

The person shipping the mediocre version wins over the person planning the perfect version. Every time.

02
Speed is a competitive advantage.

If you can ship in a week what your competitor ships in a quarter, you've won thirteen weeks of feedback they don't have.

03
Ideas are cheap. Distribution isn't.

The best product no one hears about loses to the mediocre product everyone sees. Build the audience while you build the thing.

04
Technology should remove friction.

If your product adds a step to my day, it's not helping. The best AI feels like the friction just disappeared.

05
The best products solve boring problems.

Nobody's building the twenty-first sexy AI notes app. The wins are in dispatch, scheduling, procurement, invoicing — where AI is invisible and life is easier.

06
Build for usefulness, not attention.

Attention is a rented audience. Usefulness is an owned one. I'd rather have a thousand people who need what I make than a million who watched me talk about it.
