What connects all three

Every product I build tries to remove friction from a specific person's day.

Extuter removes the “what should I study today” friction for a 17-year-old prepping for JEE. Genartml removes the “why is this task still manual” friction for an operations team. Evoluter removes the “what do I do with this feedback” friction for a UPSC aspirant.

Different markets, different tech, same principle. If the product isn't quietly saving someone real hours or real anxiety, I'm not that interested.
