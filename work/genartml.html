Genartml.AI automation, built for operators.

We build voice agents, workflow automation, and custom AI systems for teams that can't wait for enterprise software to catch up. Fixed scope. Fixed timeline. Real handover.

The problem

Most Indian and mid-market international businesses have 20+ hours a week of work that shouldn't exist: manual data entry, appointment confirmations, quote follow-ups, lead routing, invoice chasing.

They know AI could handle it. What they don't have is a team that can build it, deploy it, and hand it back running — without disappearing into a six-month consulting engagement.

Our angle

We're a small, senior team. We take on fixed-scope builds with a fixed timeline — usually four to eight weeks. We ship the working system, train your team on it, and leave real documentation.

No retainers. No “strategic AI transformation” theatre. Just: this specific problem, this specific system, this specific date.

What we build
  • Voice AI agents — inbound and outbound calls, appointment booking, lead qualification, in English & Indian languages.
  • Workflow automation — connect your CRM, sheets, email, WhatsApp into flows that run themselves.
  • Custom AI integrations — GPT / Claude / open-source models wired into your internal tools with the guardrails you actually need.
  • Chatbots that don't suck — trained on your product, docs, and past support tickets. Handoff to humans when they should.
  • Data + AI pipelines — the boring but critical plumbing that makes everything above work reliably.
Who we work with

Real estate teams. Healthcare clinics. Agritech companies. Solar installers. SMBs and mid-market brands who want to move fast and don't have time for a Big Four proposal.

We've shipped 24 builds since starting. Most clients come back for a second one within six months.

How to start

Book a call. Come with a specific process you want to automate or a specific outcome you want. In 30 minutes we'll tell you whether we can help — and if we can, what it looks like.
