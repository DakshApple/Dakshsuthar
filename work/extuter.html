01 — What Extuter is
Read: five minutes

Every JEE and NEET aspirant is drowning in the same thing: more content. More coaching, more mock tests, more PDFs, more “top 50 problem” lists.

What almost no student has is a study partner who actually knows them — where they're weak, what they got wrong last week, which chapter they've been avoiding, and how to fix it before Sunday's test.

Extuter is that partner. It watches how you solve, notices what you skip, tracks the mistakes you keep repeating, and quietly builds a study plan that adapts every day. No dashboards to check. No streaks to guilt-trip you. Just the next right thing to do.

We're building it because I remember what this preparation felt like. I don't want another kid to spend twelve hours a day feeling behind on something they can't even name.

02 — What it does
Six pieces, one flow
01

Adaptive daily plan

Every morning, Extuter tells you exactly what to study, in what order, for how long. Miss a day? It re-plans. Ace a chapter? It moves on.

02

Mistake memory

Every wrong answer is remembered — not the question, the reason. If you keep confusing s-block reactivity, it'll surface until you don't.

03

Ask, don't search

Stuck at 2 AM? Explain in your own words. Extuter answers in your syllabus context, with the exact concept your NCERT chapter uses.

04

Weekly mock, honest score

Full-syllabus tests every week. Real NTA-style scoring. Detailed breakdown of where you lost marks and — more importantly — why.

05

Parent view (opt-in)

Your parents see honest progress, not vanity streaks. Optional. Off by default. Because you're 17 and it's your account.

06

Built for offline India

Works on 3G. Under 40 MB. Downloads your daily plan overnight so your morning study doesn't wait on a signal.
