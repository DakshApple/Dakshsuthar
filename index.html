02 — The founder behind it
Ahmedabad, India

17.Building anyway.

I'm Daksh — a founder obsessed with building products, understanding people and turning ideas into systems that outlive the day I had the idea.

I grew up in a business family. My father runs his own. So I never treated entrepreneurship as a career decision — it was the air. What I chose was the tool: AI, distribution, and speed.

I dropped out of college to work on this full-time. That's not a badge; it's just the honest answer to “how do you have time?”.
